# About

## Versioning

The project's versioning follows the [SemVer](https://semver.org/) convention. However, we
exclude metrics (i.e. anything under the `:8081/metrics` endpoint), traces, and the contents of
the container image from this. Always be prepared to double-check the
[release notes](https://github.com/miracum/fhir-pseudonymizer/releases) before updating.
